Here’s a well-structured list broken down by category 👇

---

## 🎬 **A. For Video Editors**

### 🔧 **1. Technical & Software Skills**

1. Which video editing tools do you primarily use (e.g., Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut, After Effects)?
2. Can you walk me through your typical editing workflow — from importing footage to final export?
3. How do you ensure your videos are optimized for different platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok)?
4. How do you handle color correction and grading?
5. What’s your process for managing large video files and maintaining project organization?
6. Can you explain how you manage sound design and audio syncing in your edits?
7. What export settings do you typically use for web delivery?
8. How do you ensure smooth playback and performance during editing on your system?

---

### 🎨 **2. Creative & Storytelling Ability**

1. How do you decide on pacing and rhythm in a video?
2. Tell me about a project where your editing style significantly improved the final outcome.
3. How do you make your edits more engaging — transitions, effects, music choice, or narrative?
4. How do you handle creative differences with clients or team members?
5. What’s your approach to keeping the audience emotionally connected throughout a video?

---

### ⏱️ **3. Time Management & Problem-Solving**

1. How do you handle tight deadlines or last-minute changes?
2. Describe a time you had to troubleshoot a corrupted or missing project file.
3. How do you prioritize tasks when editing multiple projects at once?

---

### 🧠 **4. Knowledge of Trends & Adaptability**

1. How do you stay updated with editing trends or platform-specific video styles?
2. Which creators or production houses inspire your editing style?
3. How do you adapt your editing for short-form vs long-form content?

---

## 📱 **B. For Content Managers / Creators**

### 🧭 **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. How do you plan and organize a content calendar?
2. How do you decide what type of content performs best on a given platform?
3. Describe your process for aligning content with brand goals or audience interests.
4. How do you analyze content performance and adjust strategy based on insights?
5. How do you manage collaboration between writers, designers, and editors?

---

### 📊 **2. Platform Expertise**

1. Which platforms do you manage (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, etc.)?
2. How do you tailor content for each platform’s audience and algorithm?
3. How do you handle posting frequency and timing?
4. What analytics tools do you use to track performance?

---

### 💡 **3. Creativity & Campaign Execution**

1. Can you share an example of a content campaign you led that performed exceptionally well?
2. How do you come up with new content ideas consistently?
3. How do you maintain a consistent brand voice across all platforms?
4. How do you handle feedback or low-performing posts?

---

### 🧰 **4. Tools & Workflow**

1. What project management tools do you use (Notion, Trello, Asana, etc.)?
2. How do you collaborate with designers or video editors remotely?
3. Which AI or automation tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Canva Magic Studio, Metricool, Buffer) do you find most useful?

---

### 🗣️ **5. Communication & Brand Alignment**

1. How do you make sure the brand message stays consistent across all content formats?
2. How do you handle crisis communication or negative feedback online?
3. What’s your process for writing engaging captions or headlines?
4. How do you balance creativity with marketing goals (conversions, engagement, or awareness)?

---

## 💬 **C. Scenario-Based / Personality Questions**

1. You’re given a video that looks dull — how would you make it engaging?
2. If a campaign deadline moves up suddenly, how do you react and reorganize tasks?
3. A post underperforms despite great visuals — what do you do next?
4. How do you measure success in your role — what metrics matter most to you?
5. How do you handle creative burnout or repetitive work?

---

## 🧩 **Bonus Questions (for Both Roles)**

* What’s your proudest project so far and why?
* How do you stay inspired and avoid creative stagnation?
* What’s your process for giving or receiving feedback?
* What’s one editing or content tool you can’t live without?
* Where do you see the future of video/content creation heading?
